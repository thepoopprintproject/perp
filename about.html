About The Poop Print Project

Why I Do This

My Story

Hi, I'm a student in Geneva who's passionate about 3D printing, sustainability, and mental health support. Like many students, I've dealt with anxiety and discovered firsthand how helpful fidgets can be for managing stress and staying focused.

This project started when I noticed how much filament waste my 3D printer produced. Those small blobs and failed prints (we call them "poops" in the 3D printing community) were piling up, heading straight for the trash. Meanwhile, I was spending money on store-bought fidgets that weren't always affordable or effective.

That's when it clicked: Why not turn this waste into something useful for the community?

My Values

🌍 Environmental Responsibility

Every fidget diverts plastic waste from landfills while creating something meaningful.

💝 Community First

Mental health support shouldn't have a price tag. Everyone deserves access to tools that help.

🎓 Learning Through Giving

This project teaches me about design, community outreach, and making a real difference.

How I Ensure Quality & Safety

While these fidgets are made from "waste" material, I take safety seriously:

  • Non-toxic materials only: I use PLA filament, which is biodegradable and safe
  • Smooth edges: Every fidget is checked for sharp points or rough surfaces
  • Clean workspace: All materials are handled in a clean, organized environment
  • Age considerations: Parents should supervise young children with any fidget tool

What Makes This Different

Unlike commercial fidget toys, every piece from The Poop Print Project is:

  • Completely free – no hidden costs, no "donations required"
  • Environmentally conscious – made from genuine waste material
  • Locally made – by a Geneva student, for the Geneva community
  • Anxiety-informed – designed by someone who actually uses fidgets daily
