What I Have Printed

Creative Re-Purposing: From Filament Scraps to Sensory Comfort

Each fidget is unique, born from material that would otherwise be waste. No two are exactly alike – that's part of their charm and effectiveness.

<-----! FIDGET GALLERY !---->

Spiky Bumps & Textures

Best for: Sensory seekers, focus enhancement, tactile stimulation

These fidgets feature various textures, bumps, and ridges created from larger filament blobs. Perfect for people who need strong tactile feedback to stay calm or concentrated.

  • Varied surface textures
  • Different sizes available
  • Great for stress relief
  • Pocket-friendly designs

Smooth Spheres & Shapes

Best for: Calming, meditation, gentle stimulation

When printer scraps create smoother forms, I shape them into soothing spheres and gentle curves. These provide subtle sensory feedback without being overwhelming.

  • Calming, smooth surfaces
  • Various weights and sizes
  • Perfect for meditation
  • Anxiety-reducing designs

Geared Gems & Moving Parts

Best for: Active fidgeters, mechanical satisfaction, focus

Some scraps lend themselves perfectly to creating small mechanical elements – gears that turn, parts that slide, or elements that click together satisfyingly.

  • Moving mechanical parts
  • Satisfying clicks and rotations
  • Engaging for busy hands
  • ADHD-friendly designs

Organic Blobs & Abstract Forms

Best for: Creative minds, unique textures, conversation starters

Sometimes the most interesting fidgets come from embracing the natural, chaotic shapes that printer waste creates. These organic forms offer unexpected textures and grip points.

  • Completely unique shapes
  • Natural, organic feel
  • Unexpected texture combinations
  • Great conversation pieces

🌱Support the Project

This project started small — just me, my 3D printer, and a few extra spools of filament. Instead of letting scraps go to waste, I began turning them into calming fidgets and sharing them with friends and the community.

By supporting this project, you help cover the costs of materials and keep it sustainable. Every contribution makes it possible to:

  • Print more fidgets to share for free in Geneva.
  • Experiment with new designs and colors.
  • Expand the reach so more people can benefit.

Together, we can spread calm, one fidget at a time.
